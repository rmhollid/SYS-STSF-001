# Contributing to SYS-STSF-001

Contributions are welcome, but repository contribution does not itself modify STSF authority.

## Before submitting

- Keep changes narrowly scoped.
- Do not silently alter canonical STD, PRG, SPL, bindings, manifests, hashes, or evidence.
- Preserve attribution, provenance, version identifiers, and integrity records.
- Clearly distinguish repository-maintenance changes from normative STSF changes.
- Do not claim compliance, validation, certification, or authority without the required evidence.

## Licensing

By submitting a contribution, you represent that you have the right to submit it.

Unless a separate written agreement applies, submitted contributions are offered for inclusion under the repository's current licensing model. Commercial licensing remains reserved separately.

## Canonical changes

Changes to normative STSF material require the applicable STSF governance and validation process. A pull request alone is not authority.
