# Commercial Licensing

STSF is publicly available for noncommercial use under the repository's public noncommercial license.

Commercial use requires a separate paid written license from the applicable STSF rights holder.

## Commercial use examples

A commercial license is required for uses such as:

- shipping STSF in a paid product;
- using STSF as part of a paid service;
- commercial hosting or managed operation;
- paid consulting or implementation based materially on STSF;
- commercial redistribution or relicensing;
- embedding STSF into proprietary commercial tooling;
- material use of STSF in revenue-generating production operations.

## Licensing inquiries

Open a GitHub issue titled **Commercial Licensing Inquiry** and provide a high-level description of the intended use. Do not include confidential business information in a public issue.

Commercial terms, scope, duration, distribution rights, support, and pricing are negotiated separately.

The public repository does not itself grant commercial rights.
